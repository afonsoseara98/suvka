# SUVKA CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.1

---

# Mission

Build the world's best AI platform for generating landing pages that maximize conversions.

Suvka does not compete on speed.

Suvka competes on intelligence, quality, and measurable conversion performance.

Every landing page should feel as if it was created by a senior conversion strategist, senior copywriter, senior UX designer, and senior front-end engineer working together.

---

# Vision

Suvka is not an AI website builder.

Suvka is an AI Conversion System.

The landing page is the final output of a structured reasoning process.

Our competitive advantage is not generation.

Our competitive advantage is decision-making.

---

# Product Philosophy

The AI must think before it generates.

Generation is the final step.

Planning is the product.

Every decision must increase one or more of the following:

- Conversion
- Clarity
- Trust
- Maintainability
- Scalability

---

# Engineering Principles

1. Quality over speed.
2. Strong typing everywhere.
3. Modular architecture.
4. One responsibility per module.
5. Data-driven decisions.
6. No duplicated logic.
7. AI plans first.
8. AI generates second.
9. AI validates third.
10. Everything must be replaceable.
11. Every module must be independently testable.
12. Business logic never belongs inside UI components.
13. Structured data always comes before prompting an LLM.
14. Every architectural decision must improve either conversion or maintainability.

---

# AI Principles

The LLM is not the product.

The pipeline is the product.

The AI should never improvise information when structured knowledge exists.

The AI reasons using structured objects—not raw prompts.

Every module enriches the business understanding before generation.

# Core Engines

The Suvka architecture is divided into independent engines.

## Business Intelligence Engine

Responsible for understanding:

- Industry
- Business model
- Target audience
- Goals
- Pricing
- Positioning
- Tone

---

## Psychology Engine

Responsible for identifying:

- Pain points
- Desires
- Objections
- Trust factors
- Emotional triggers
- Buying motivations

---

## Knowledge Engine

Responsible for enriching the business with industry-specific knowledge.

Examples:

- Medical
- Legal
- SaaS
- Restaurant
- Fitness
- Finance

---

## Conversion Engine

Responsible for defining:

- Offer
- CTA strategy
- Urgency
- Social proof
- Guarantees
- Section ordering
- Conversion flow

---

## Copy Engine

Responsible only for writing.

It never decides strategy.

It executes the strategy produced by previous engines.

---

## Design Engine

Responsible for:

- Theme
- Colors
- Typography
- Layout
- Spacing
- Visual hierarchy
- Component styling

---

## Component Engine

Responsible for selecting and composing UI components.

It chooses the best component based on business context rather than fixed templates.

---

## Rendering Engine

Responsible for transforming structured JSON into React components.

Rendering must remain deterministic.

---

## Validation Engine

Responsible for validating:

- Accessibility
- Consistency
- Missing sections
- Broken hierarchy
- Design issues
- Copy quality

---

## Scoring Engine

Responsible for assigning measurable scores:

- Conversion
- UX
- SEO
- Accessibility
- Performance
- Readability
- Trust

It must also generate improvement suggestions.

---

# Architectural Rules

Never generate HTML directly.

Never hardcode industries.

Never duplicate prompts.

Never duplicate business logic.

Never place business logic inside React components.

Never allow modules to have multiple responsibilities.

Prefer composition over inheritance.

Prefer configuration over hardcoded logic.

Every module must have a single responsibility.

Every module must have typed inputs and outputs.

Every decision must be reproducible.

---

# Definition of Done

A feature is considered complete only if it:

- Has a clear responsibility.
- Is strongly typed.
- Is documented.
- Is reusable.
- Improves either conversion or architecture.
- Does not introduce duplicated logic.
- Integrates cleanly into the AI pipeline.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Create the most intelligent landing page generation platform in the world.

Suvka should consistently produce landing pages that outperform manually created pages in:

- Conversion
- Design quality
- Copy quality
- UX
- SEO
- Maintainability

The objective is not to generate pages faster.

The objective is to generate pages that convert better.
